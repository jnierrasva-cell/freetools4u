About FreeTools4U

Practical online utilities designed to make everyday digital tasks simpler.

What we build

FreeTools4U is a growing collection of small, focused utilities for common tasks such as counting and transforming text, calculating percentages and dates, formatting JSON, and generating passwords. Each tool is designed around a specific job so visitors can get to the task without creating an account or installing desktop software.

How the tools work

Most text, calculation, date, and JSON utilities run directly in your browser. Their input is processed on your device and is not sent to our website servers. The PDF to Word converter is different: the PDF must be sent to our conversion service so it can produce a DOCX file. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Our approach

  • Clear instructions and useful results over unnecessary features.
  • Simple interfaces that work on desktop and mobile screens.
  • Transparent explanations of what each tool can and cannot do.
  • Ongoing improvements based on testing and visitor feedback.

Accuracy and limitations

Tools are intended for general productivity and informational use. Results can depend on input quality, browser behavior, date conventions, and the assumptions explained on each tool page. Please verify important calculations and keep secure backups of important information.
