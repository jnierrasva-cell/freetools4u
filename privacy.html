Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FreeTools4U handles information when you visit this website and use its tools. It describes the current site functionality. If we add analytics, accounts, contact forms, or other data collection features, this policy will be updated before or when those features are introduced.

1. Information you enter into browser-based tools

Most tools on FreeTools4U—including the text, calculator, date, JSON, and password tools—run locally in your browser. The content you enter into these tools is processed on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to our application server by those tools. Avoid using shared or public devices for sensitive work, and clear the page when you finish if you do not want the information to remain visible in the open browser tab.

2. PDF to Word conversion

The PDF to Word tool requires you to upload a PDF to our conversion API hosted by Render. The service temporarily writes the uploaded PDF and generated DOCX file to a temporary server directory to perform the conversion. The application attempts to delete both temporary files after processing, whether conversion succeeds or fails. As with any server-side processing, unexpected infrastructure interruption may prevent normal cleanup. Do not upload documents containing information you are not comfortable sending to a third-party hosting and processing service.

The conversion service may log operational information such as the uploaded filename and file size to help diagnose conversion activity. The website does not provide an account-based file library, and the conversion tool does not intentionally retain completed documents for later retrieval.

3. Hosting, fonts, and service providers

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages and the PDF conversion API is hosted by Render. Pages may load fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request data as part of delivering their services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Cookies, advertising, and analytics

FreeTools4U may display third-party advertising, including Google AdSense, if advertising is enabled. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize ads, subject to their settings and applicable law. We do not ask visitors to click advertisements. At the time of this policy update, no first-party analytics or account system is described as part of the site's tool functionality. If advertising or analytics technologies are activated, the applicable provider disclosures and consent controls will be made available where required.

5. Data security

We use the safeguards available through our hosting and application setup, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Do not submit highly confidential, financial, medical, or legally sensitive documents to the PDF converter unless you have assessed the risks and are authorized to do so.

6. Children's privacy

FreeTools4U is a general-purpose utility website and is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the tools.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to use a tool, close the page, or avoid uploading a document. Browser-local tool inputs are under your control on your device. Requests about information handled by third-party providers may need to be directed to those providers under their policies. Contact information for site privacy requests will be published on this page when the site's support channel is finalized.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site changes. The revision date above indicates the latest update. Continued use of the website after a policy update means you have had the opportunity to review the revised policy.
