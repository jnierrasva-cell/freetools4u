What this age calculator does

Calculate a person's age in completed years, months, and days on a selected date. It can also calculate an age on a past or future date for planning and record checks.

How to use it

Choose the date of birth and the date to calculate age on. Select Calculate age. If you leave the calculation date as today, the tool uses the date shown in your browser.

Important details and limitations

The result uses calendar dates, not the exact time of birth. Leap years and month lengths are accounted for by calendar arithmetic. Confirm important age requirements against the official rules of the organization requesting the information.
