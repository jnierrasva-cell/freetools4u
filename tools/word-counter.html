What this word counter does

Count words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and estimated reading time in a text passage. Useful for essays, website copy, social posts, and content briefs.

How to use it

Paste or type text into the editor. The counters update as you type. Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute; actual reading speed varies. Sentence counts are estimated from punctuation, so abbreviations and unusual formatting can affect the result.

Important details and limitations

Word count treats whitespace-separated groups as words. Character count includes spaces and line breaks. Paragraphs are separated by one or more blank lines. The tool does not upload your text.
