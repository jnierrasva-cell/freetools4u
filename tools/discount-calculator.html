What this discount calculator does

Calculate the amount saved and the final price after a percentage discount. Useful when comparing sale prices, checking promotional offers, or planning a purchase.

How to use it

Enter the original price and a discount from 0 to 100 percent, then select Calculate. The result shows the sale price and the amount saved, rounded to two decimal places.

Important details and limitations

This calculator applies one percentage discount to one price. It does not include taxes, shipping, coupons applied in sequence, currency conversion, or store-specific rounding rules.
