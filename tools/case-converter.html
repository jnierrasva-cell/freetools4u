What this case converter does

Change text capitalization without retyping. Convert a draft into uppercase, lowercase, title case, or sentence case for headings, labels, and everyday writing.

How to use it

Paste your text, choose the target capitalization, then select Convert. Review the result before copying it into your document. Title case capitalizes word starts; sentence case capitalizes the first letter after sentence-ending punctuation.

Important details and limitations

Capitalization rules differ between style guides and languages. This simple converter is intended for English-like text and does not apply specialist editorial rules or preserve every proper-noun exception.
