📖 How to use this tool

  • 1
    For Browser Tabs (Best Experience) Click "Select Audio to Boost", choose the Chrome Tab playing your video/music, and ensure "Share tab audio" is checked.
  • 2
    For Local Software (VLC, OBS, Games) Click "Select Audio to Boost", choose "Entire Screen", and check "Share system audio" at the bottom.
⚠️ Important for Local Software: Browsers mute the captured audio to prevent digital feedback loops (that "machine noise"). If you are capturing "Entire Screen", the audio will play normally on your computer, but the volume sliders on this page will not affect what you hear unless you use a virtual audio cable (like VB-Audio). This tool works perfectly and applies live boosts for Browser Tabs.
